Claude Fable 5: What Changed and Why It Matters

A note from me: Fable 5 is the narrative-tuned member of the Claude 5 family, sitting alongside Sonnet 5 and Opus 4.8 with a different set of tradeoffs. Here's how I think about where it fits.

💪 Where it's strongest

Long-form creative writing, character consistency across a long draft, and dialogue that doesn't flatten out after a few pages.

🚫 Where it isn't the right pick

Heavy multi-step coding tasks or anything that needs tool use at scale — reach for Sonnet 5 for that instead.

🧭 Picking a model

If the task is "write" → lean Fable.
If the task is "build" → lean Sonnet.
If the task is "reason carefully about something hard" → lean Opus.
