Claude Fable 5: What Changed and Why It Matters

Hey, it's Riz 👋 Fable 5 is the narrative-tuned member of the Claude 5 family, and it's not the right pick for everything. Here's where it shines, where it doesn't, and how I decide between it, Sonnet, and Opus.

Where it's strongest 💪

  • ✅ Long-form creative writing
  • ✅ Character consistency across a long draft
  • ✅ Dialogue that doesn't flatten out after a few pages

Where it isn't the right pick 🚫

  • 🚫 Heavy multi-step coding tasks
  • 🚫 Anything that needs tool use at scale — reach for Sonnet 5 for that instead

Picking a model 🧭

If the task is "write" → lean Fable.
If the task is "build" → lean Sonnet.
If the task is "reason carefully about something hard" → lean Opus.
